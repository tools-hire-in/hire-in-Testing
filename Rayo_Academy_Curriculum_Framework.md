# Rayo Academy — Certification Curriculum Framework
  ### Prepared by: Rayomind Solutions — Architecture & Product
  ### Version: 1.0 | Confidential — Internal Use Only

  ---

  ## Overview

  Rayo Academy delivers three certification programs for staffing and talent acquisition professionals. All certifications are built on a prerequisite chain that ensures every specialist earns a common foundation before entering a domain track.

  ```
  CFC (Common Foundation Certification) — mandatory for all
  ├── CHTP (Certified Healthcare TA Performance) — healthcare specialist track
  └── CITP (Certified IT TA Performance) — IT specialist track
  ```

  Each track has a 4-tier progression. Tiers require demonstrated tenure and performance — progression is not automatic or time-based. Completing a tier earns a verifiable digital certificate, XP, and a badge.

  ---

  ## Part 1: CFC — Common Foundation Certification

  ### Purpose
  Every TA professional at any company using Rayo Academy must complete the CFC before entering a specialist track. The CFC establishes the professional identity, mindset, communication standard, and process discipline that everything else builds on.

  ### Hard Rule
  No one may begin CHTP or CITP without holding a current CFC certification.

  ### Module Prerequisite Chain
  ```
  CFC-101 (entry point)
      ↓
  CFC-102 + CFC-103 (parallel, both require CFC-101)
      ↓
  CFC-104 (requires both CFC-102 and CFC-103)
      ↓
  CFC-105 + CFC-106 (parallel, both require CFC-104)
      ↓
  CFC Final Assessment → CFC Certified (+500 XP + certificate)
  ```

  ### Module Catalog

  **CFC-101: Company Identity, Culture & TA Expert Mindset**
  - Duration: 3 hours | XP: +100 | Prerequisite: None
  - Learning objectives: Define the TA Expert identity vs. transactional recruiter. Understand the candidate care standard. Know the escalation standard and communication policy. Apply the Rayo Academy / Rayomind Solutions mission to daily decisions.
  - Scenario Challenge "The TA Expert Mindset Test": Candidate matches on keywords but admits no genuine interest in the role. Hiring manager is desperate. Do you submit? TA Expert does not submit — keyword match without genuine interest sets up early termination.
  - Quiz: 5 questions on TA Expert identity, candidate care principles, escalation triggers

  **CFC-102: Candidate Care Standard & Relationship Philosophy**
  - Duration: 2 hours | XP: +100 | Prerequisite: CFC-101
  - Learning objectives: Treat candidates as professionals, not inventory. Apply the candidate relationship lifecycle (first call → placement → redeployment → referral). Deliver closure messages. Maintain controlled persistence without spam.
  - Scenario Challenge "The Communication Test": Candidate hasn't responded by Wednesday. Requisition is urgent. Right move = one final low-pressure contact attempt, document, move on.
  - Quiz: 5 questions on cadence, closure messaging, relationship standards

  **CFC-103: Companywide Policies & Communication Standards**
  - Duration: 2 hours | XP: +100 | Prerequisite: CFC-101
  - Learning objectives: Microsoft Teams as the sole communication channel. Documentation discipline (if not written, it didn't happen). Escalation triggers and format. Confidentiality and data handling.
  - Scenario Challenge "The Escalation Test": Colleague asks you to stay quiet about a candidate with a compliance concern already submitted to client. TA Expert escalates — credential integrity is non-negotiable.
  - Quiz: 5 questions on documentation, escalation format, confidentiality

  **CFC-104: Recruitment Lifecycle & Definition of Done**
  - Duration: 3 hours | XP: +100 | Prerequisite: CFC-102 + CFC-103
  - Learning objectives: Know the 6-stage recruitment lifecycle end-to-end (Intake, Sourcing, Screening, Submission, Interview, Close/Offer). Apply the Definition of Done at each stage. Identify what makes a stage incomplete.
  - Stage 1 Intake Definition of Done: TA Expert can pitch the role in 30 seconds and explain why it's hard to fill.
  - Stage 2 Sourcing Definition of Done: Workable candidate pool built, outreach documented.
  - Stage 3 Screening Definition of Done: Structured checklist complete, career goals understood, compensation aligned.
  - Stage 4 Submission Definition of Done: Fit narrative written, credential status confirmed, risks documented.
  - Stage 5 Interview Definition of Done: Candidate prepped on process, format, and key questions. Client expectations reset if needed.
  - Stage 6 Close Definition of Done: Offer accepted, counteroffer risk assessed, start date confirmed, day-one instructions sent.
  - Quiz: 6 questions, one per stage, on Definition of Done criteria

  **CFC-105: Communication Excellence**
  - Duration: 3 hours | XP: +100 | Prerequisite: CFC-104
  - Learning objectives: Deliver first-time call with proper structure (research first, 30-second pitch, ask one question). Write voicemail under 25 seconds, clear and low-pressure. SMS messaging guidelines. Email structure for status updates and follow-up. Handle 5 common objections.
  - Objection matrix covered: "I'm not actively looking" / "The rate doesn't work" / "I need to think about it" / "I have another offer" / "I need to talk to my spouse"
  - Scenario Challenge: Candidate says "I'll listen but I'm not actively looking." How do you handle the next 90 seconds without pressure?
  - Quiz: 5 questions on scripts, SMS cadence, objection handling

  **CFC-106: Quality Standards, Documentation & Systems Setup**
  - Duration: 2 hours | XP: +100 | Prerequisite: CFC-104
  - Learning objectives: Know the 8 required elements of a complete submission. Apply documentation discipline in the ATS. Identify resume red flags (keyword stuffing, inflated claims, inconsistent timelines).
  - Red Flag Spotter exercise: Given 3 candidate profiles, identify all red flags.
  - 8 required submission elements: Full name + contact | Resume (current, clean) | Recruiter summary (fit narrative, not bio) | License/credential status (healthcare) or work auth (IT) | Compensation alignment confirmed | Availability and start date | Risks documented | Client-ready package
  - Quiz: 5 questions on submission quality, red flags, ATS hygiene

  ### CFC Final Assessment
  - 25 questions spanning all 6 modules
  - Passing threshold: 80%
  - Reward: +500 XP + CFC Certified badge + digital certificate (RAYO-CFC-YYYY-XXXX)
  - Certificate is publicly verifiable at rayo.academy/verify/[code]

  ## Part 2: CHTP — Certified Healthcare TA Performance

  ### Overview
  The CHTP builds specialist healthcare recruiting depth on top of the CFC foundation. A practitioner who holds CHTP Tier 1 can source, screen, and submit healthcare candidates with genuine domain fluency — understanding specialties, compliance requirements, credentialing timelines, and healthcare-specific communication.

  ### Prerequisite: CFC Certification

  ### Four-Tier Structure

  | Tier | Title | Entry Requirement | XP Available |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Tier 1 | Healthcare TA Foundations | CFC Certified | 1,250 XP |
  | Tier 2 | Healthcare TA Advanced | Tier 1 + 3 months + performance baseline | 1,400 XP |
  | Tier 3 | Healthcare TA Senior | Tier 2 + 6 months + top-quartile scorecard | 1,600 XP |
  | Tier 4 | Healthcare TA Lead | Tier 3 + 12 months + leadership demonstrated | 1,800 XP |

  ### Tier 1 Module Catalog

  **CHTP-101: Healthcare Industry Fundamentals**
  - Duration: 4 hours | XP: +100 | Prerequisite: CFC Certified
  - Learning objectives: Identify healthcare role families (RN specialties: ICU, ER, Med-Surg, OR, PACU, L&D, NICU, Telemetry; LPN/LVN; CNA; Allied Health; Lab; Imaging; Pharmacy; APP). Distinguish assignment types (travel 13-week, local contract, per diem, permanent). Understand care settings and shift models.
  - Scenario "The Specialty Mismatch": Client needs ICU RN with Level 1 trauma experience. Candidate has 3 years Med-Surg and says she can handle ICU. Do you submit?

  **CHTP-102: Healthcare Compliance, Licensure & Credentialing**
  - Duration: 4 hours | XP: +100 | Prerequisite: CHTP-101
  - Learning objectives: Verify active license through state boards. Understand compact vs. single-state licensure. Confirm required certifications (BLS, ACLS, PALS, NRP, TNCC, NIHSS). Pre-employment requirements (background, drug screen, immunizations, TB, physical). Credentialing timeline implications.
  - Scenario "The Expired Certification": ICU RN with ACLS expired 2 months ago, plans to renew soon, start date in 10 days. Can you submit?

  **CHTP-103: Healthcare Recruitment SOP & Definition of Done**
  - Duration: 3 hours | XP: +100 | Prerequisite: CHTP-102
  - Learning objectives: Master the 6-stage healthcare SOP with healthcare-specific Definition of Done at each stage. Identify what makes a healthcare submission incomplete. Recognize healthcare red flags.
  - Scenario "The Incomplete Submission": Manager asks you to submit quickly for urgent travel nursing role. You have screened by phone but not verified license, confirmed certs, or discussed comp. Do you submit to meet the deadline?

  **CHTP-104: Healthcare Communication, Outreach & Screening**
  - Duration: 3 hours | XP: +100 | Prerequisite: CHTP-103
  - Learning objectives: Deliver healthcare-specific call, voicemail, and SMS scripts. Present roles leading with specialty, shift, location, credential requirements. Screen using structured healthcare checklist. Handle objections around shift, float, location, pay packages.
  - Live Drill: Deliver first-time call, voicemail, and SMS for Travel ICU RN role, Phoenix AZ, nights, 13 weeks.

  **CHTP-105: Healthcare Submission Quality & Documentation**
  - Duration: 2 hours | XP: +100 | Prerequisite: CHTP-104
  - Learning objectives: Prepare complete healthcare submission package. Write recruiter summary for healthcare candidates. Document credential status, availability, compensation alignment, and risks.
  - Submission Showdown: Two learners prepare competing submissions for the same healthcare req. Manager evaluates which is more complete, honest, and client-ready. Winner: +200 XP.

  ### Tiers 2–4 Module Catalog (Summary)

  **Tier 2 — Healthcare TA Advanced**
  - CHTP-201: Advanced Healthcare Credentialing and Compliance
  - CHTP-202: Healthcare Consultative Intake and Client Advisory
  - CHTP-203: Healthcare Negotiation: Pay Packages and Close
  - CHTP-204: Healthcare Post-Placement Care and Redeployment

  **Tier 3 — Healthcare TA Senior**
  - CHTP-301: Healthcare Market Dynamics and Specialty Depth
  - CHTP-302: Complex Healthcare Close Scenarios
  - CHTP-303: Healthcare Client Partnership and Account Growth
  - CHTP-304: Healthcare Mentoring and Knowledge Transfer

  **Tier 4 — Healthcare TA Lead**
  - CHTP-401: Healthcare Team Leadership and Performance Coaching
  - CHTP-402: Strategic Healthcare Account Management
  - CHTP-403: Healthcare Training Design and Delivery
  - CHTP-404: Healthcare Business Development Fundamentals

  ## Part 3: CITP — Certified IT TA Performance

  ### Overview
  The CITP builds specialist IT recruiting depth on top of the CFC. A CITP-certified TA professional can speak credibly to IT candidates about their stack, validate technical depth without coding, screen accurately for must-have vs. nice-to-have, and write submission summaries that demonstrate genuine role understanding.

  ### Prerequisite: CFC Certification

  ### Four-Tier Structure

  | Tier | Title | Entry Requirement | XP Available |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Tier 1 | IT TA Foundations | CFC Certified | 1,250 XP |
  | Tier 2 | IT TA Advanced | Tier 1 + 3 months + performance baseline | 1,400 XP |
  | Tier 3 | IT TA Senior | Tier 2 + 6 months + top-quartile scorecard | 1,600 XP |
  | Tier 4 | IT TA Lead | Tier 3 + 12 months + leadership demonstrated | 1,800 XP |

  ### Tier 1 Module Catalog

  **CITP-101: IT Industry Fundamentals & Role Families**
  - Duration: 4 hours | XP: +100 | Prerequisite: CFC Certified
  - Learning objectives: Identify IT role families (Software Engineering: frontend, backend, full-stack, mobile; QA; Data engineering/science/analytics/ML; DevOps/SRE; Cloud; Security; Product; ERP; Support; UX). Understand engagement types (W2, Corp-to-Corp, CTH, full-time). Understand work authorization categories (US Citizen, Green Card, H-1B, OPT, CPT, EAD, L1).
  - Scenario "The Role Family Confusion": Client asks for Senior Full Stack Developer with React, Node.js, PostgreSQL, AWS. Candidate has 8 years .NET/C# with SQL Server and Azure and calls himself Full Stack. Is this a match?

  **CITP-102: Technology Stack Awareness & Technical Fluency**
  - Duration: 4 hours | XP: +100 | Prerequisite: CITP-101
  - Learning objectives: Understand common tech groupings (frontend, backend, database, cloud, DevOps, data, QA). Distinguish similar-sounding technologies (Java vs JavaScript, SQL vs NoSQL, React vs Angular). Recognize keyword-listing vs. genuine hands-on depth on a resume. Ask basic technical validation questions without coding knowledge.
  - Red Flag Spotter: Given 3 IT candidate resumes, identify all red flags: keyword stuffing, old tech listed as current, inflated ownership claims, inconsistent timelines, vague role descriptions.

  **CITP-103: IT Recruitment SOP, Authorization & Definition of Done**
  - Duration: 3 hours | XP: +100 | Prerequisite: CITP-102
  - Learning objectives: Master the 6-stage IT SOP. Separate must-have from nice-to-have skills at intake. Confirm work authorization before submission. Apply the IT Definition of Done at each stage.

  **CITP-104: IT Communication, Outreach & Technical Screening**
  - Duration: 3 hours | XP: +100 | Prerequisite: CITP-103
  - Learning objectives: Deliver IT-specific call, voicemail, and SMS scripts that sound informed (not generic). Present roles leading with stack relevance, work model, and project context. Screen IT candidates for actual hands-on depth, not resume keywords. Handle objections around remote work, compensation, and authorization.
  - Live Drill: Deliver first-time call, voicemail, and SMS for Senior QA Automation Engineer, remote PST, requiring Playwright and API testing.

  **CITP-105: IT Submission Quality & Recruiter Summary Writing**
  - Duration: 2 hours | XP: +100 | Prerequisite: CITP-104
  - Learning objectives: Write recruiter summaries that explain why the candidate fits, not just what they have done. Highlight must-have alignment specifically. Document risks honestly.
  - Submission Showdown: Two learners prepare competing IT submissions. Winner: +200 XP.

  ## Part 4: Performance Framework

  ### Quarterly Training Assignment Workflow

  Every quarter, each manager reviews their team's scorecard and assigns 1–3 modules. Assignment is data-driven, not arbitrary.

  **Manager decision process:**
  1. Review scorecard — identify 2–3 weakest metrics per team member
  2. Map metrics to training modules using the table below
  3. Check tier progression status — if eligible for next tier, assign those modules
  4. Document the assignment with reason, module, deadline, expected outcome
  5. Conduct coaching session after completion, update scorecard notes

  **Scorecard-to-Module Mapping:**

  | Weak Metric | Root Cause | Assign Module |
  |---|---|---|
  | Low submission quality | Weak screening or poor recruiter summaries | CFC-106 or CHTP/CITP-105 |
  | Low submittal-to-interview ratio | Poor requirement understanding or matching | CFC-104 or CHTP/CITP-103 |
  | Low interview-to-offer ratio | Weak candidate prep or fit assessment | CFC-105 or CHTP/CITP-104 |
  | Low offer-to-start ratio | Weak candidate control or negotiation | CHTP/CITP-203 or CFC-105 |
  | Low post-placement touchpoints | No follow-through habit | CHTP/CITP-204 |
  | Low documentation completeness | Discipline gap | CFC-103 or CFC-106 |
  | Low candidate experience score | Communication or care gaps | CFC-102 or CFC-105 |
  | Healthcare credential issues | Compliance knowledge gap | CHTP-202 |
  | IT authorization rejections | Work authorization process gap | CITP-203 |

  ### Performance Review Integration

  Training carries 20% weight in quarterly performance reviews.

  | Component | Weight |
  |---|---|
  | Scorecard metrics (quality + conversion) | 40% |
  | Certification completion + assessment quality | 20% |
  | Communication and professionalism | 15% |
  | Candidate relationship and post-placement care | 15% |
  | Team contribution and collaboration | 10% |

  ### Manager Self-Certification Requirement

  Managers must complete all Tier 1 and Tier 2 certifications within their first 6 months in role. A manager cannot credibly assign training they have not completed themselves. Manager certification status is reviewed by leadership quarterly.
