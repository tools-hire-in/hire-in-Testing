# Market Research & Lead Generation — Hire'in Solutions

## Lead Research Framework

### Step 1 — Define the Target Profile

Before researching, establish the ICP (Ideal Client Profile) for the outreach campaign:

| Attribute | Signals to look for |
|---|---|
| **Industry** | Healthcare, IT, Engineering, Professional Services |
| **Size** | 100–5,000 employees (sweet spot for external staffing) |
| **Hiring velocity** | 5+ open roles in target function |
| **Growth signal** | Funding, expansion, new product launch, M&A |
| **Geography** | All 50 US states; prioritize CA, TX, NY, FL, IL, WA for volume |
| **Staffing model fit** | Contract-heavy (tech startups, consulting) or perm-heavy (healthcare, finance) |

---

### Step 2 — Identify Hiring Signals

These are the triggers that tell you a company is actively needing staffing help:

**Job Posting Volume**
- 5+ open roles in the same function in the past 30 days = high urgency
- Same role posted multiple times = they're struggling to fill it
- Tools: LinkedIn Jobs, Indeed, Glassdoor, Builtin, Lever/Greenhouse postings

**Company News**
- Series B/C/D funding announcement → aggressive hiring planned
- New office/facility opening → headcount expansion
- Product launch or partnership → engineering/sales hiring surge
- M&A activity → integration teams needed (HR, operations, IT)
- Government contract award → often requires rapid staffing ramp

**LinkedIn Company Pages**
- "Our team grew 30% last year" type posts
- Hiring Manager posts about specific open roles
- Job postings shared by employees
- Company following staffing/HR accounts = they're thinking about it

**Pain Signals**
- Glassdoor reviews mentioning "short-staffed," "understaffed," "high turnover"
- Indeed reviews: "management is stretched thin," "need more people"
- Healthcare: CMS star ratings dropping = staffing quality problems

### Step 3 — Build the Target List

For each target, document:

```
Company: [Name]
Contact: [Name, Title] — [LinkedIn URL]
Trigger: [What signal you saw]
Domain: [IT / Healthcare / Engineering / Prof. Services]
Open Roles: [Titles + count]
Outreach angle: [One sentence: what pain you'll address]
Email / Phone: [If findable]
Priority: [High / Medium / Low]
```

**Target titles to reach:**
- **IT**: VP of Engineering, Director of Engineering, CTO, Head of Talent Acquisition, Tech Recruiter
- **Healthcare**: Chief Nursing Officer, VP of Clinical Operations, HR Director, Staffing Coordinator
- **Engineering**: Director of Engineering, Plant Manager, VP of Operations, HR Business Partner
- **Professional Services**: CFO, HR Director, COO, VP of Finance

### Step 4 — Local & Regional Market Analysis

When entering a new geography or advising a client on market conditions, use this method:

**Supply Side (Talent)**
- LinkedIn Talent Insights (if available) or manual search: filter by [role title] + [city/metro] + "Open to work"
- Estimate active candidate pool size
- Check average years of experience available at target pay band
- Note universities and bootcamps in market (for junior talent pipeline)

**Demand Side (Competition)**
- Count job postings for target role in the metro over past 30/60/90 days
- Identify top employers absorbing talent (FAANG, large hospital systems, defense contractors)
- Check if local employers are using staffing firms already (Glassdoor: "hired through agency")

**Rate Benchmarks**
- Refer to the rate ranges in `references/proposals-rates.md`
- Regional adjustments: SF/NYC/Seattle = add 15–20% to midpoint; mid-market (Austin, Denver, Atlanta) = at midpoint; rural/Midwest = subtract 10–15%
- Healthcare rates track with facility type: Level 1 trauma centers pay premium; SNFs/LTACs are at the lower end

**Competitor Positioning**
- Major national staffers (Robert Half, Kforce, TEKsystems, Aerotek) compete on volume; differentiate on specialization and AI speed
- Regional boutiques compete on relationships; differentiate on technology and 24-hr SLA
- Never badmouth competitors by name in outreach; position Hire'in as the AI-augmented alternative

### Step 5 — Prioritization Matrix

Score each lead 1–3 on each dimension:

| Dimension | 1 (Low) | 2 (Medium) | 3 (High) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hiring urgency | 1–2 open roles | 3–5 open roles | 6+ open roles / recurring need |
| Growth signal | No recent news | Minor hiring growth | Funding/expansion/new contract |
| Fit with HIS | Unrelated domain | Adjacent domain | Core domain match |
| Reachability | No contact found | LinkedIn only | Email + phone found |
| Decision authority | Recruiter/coordinator | HR manager | VP/Director/C-suite |

**Score 11–15**: High priority — outreach within 24 hours
**Score 7–10**: Medium — batch in next campaign wave
**Score < 7**: Low — add to nurture list; revisit in 60 days

## Staffing Market Intelligence — Key Trends (Reference Points)

These are evergreen framing points for Hire'in positioning. Use as context in outreach and calls.

**IT Staffing**
- AI/ML roles are the fastest-growing category; demand outpaces supply by 3:1 in most markets
- Contract IT hiring is rebounding after 2023–2024 slowdown; contract-to-hire is up
- Companies are moving away from offshore-only IT staffing toward hybrid US+offshore models
- Cybersecurity and cloud roles have the longest fill times (avg 45–60 days) — prime Hire'in opportunity

**Healthcare Staffing**
- Post-pandemic travel nurse rate normalization: per-diem and local contract growing again
- Allied health (PT, OT, respiratory) shortage is as acute as nursing — often overlooked
- Credentialing is the #1 reason healthcare placements slow down — this is CredentialRx.ai's core value
- Rural healthcare systems are chronically understaffed and price-sensitive; telehealth roles span all geographies

**Engineering**
- Infrastructure bill (IIJA) driving demand for civil and structural engineers through 2030
- EV transition creating demand for electrical/power engineers and battery systems roles
- Manufacturing reshoring increasing demand for industrial and process engineers
- Project managers (PMP-certified) are in short supply across all engineering sub-domains

**Professional Services**
- Finance/accounting demand spiked around IPO cycles and regulatory changes
- HR Business Partners are in demand as companies restructure post-layoff
- Operations/supply chain roles remain elevated post-pandemic
- Marketing talent is increasingly contract-first as budgets tighten
