## Buyer Personas

### CFO / VP of Finance

**What they care about**: Business velocity, cost control, accurate financial reporting, compliance, and access to seasoned finance professionals who hit the ground running.

**Pain points**:
- Audit season creates acute short-term demand for accountants and controllers
- IPO prep / M&A diligence spikes finance headcount needs unpredictably
- Finding CPAs or finance leaders who understand their industry (healthcare finance vs. tech finance vs. manufacturing finance)
- Risk of a bad hire in a fiduciary role

**How to approach**:
- Emphasize industry-specific finance experience, not just function
- Offer contract-to-hire as a risk mitigation for senior finance roles
- Mention that KleriQ.ai scores for domain experience match, not just job title match

**Sample opener**:
> "Finance teams often face audit season or M&A diligence with not enough hands. We've placed controllers, FP&A managers, and accounting leads for companies in exactly that situation — pre-vetted, available in days, not weeks. Worth a quick call before your next crunch?"

---

### CHRO / HR Director

**What they care about**: HR operational capacity, compliance, HRIS effectiveness, building a strong TA pipeline, reducing HR-department churn.

**Pain points**:
- HR is often under-resourced and asked to do more with fewer people
- HRIS implementations create demand for system-specific talent (Workday, ADP, SAP SuccessFactors)
- TA teams stretched thin during growth phases — need recruiting support
- Difficult to find HRBPs with genuine change management experience

**How to approach**:
- Position HIS as the trusted HR talent partner who understands the function's unique pressures
- Offer to fill TA capacity gaps with contract recruiters during hiring surges
- Reference HRIS-specific talent pipeline for implementation projects

---

### COO / VP of Operations

**What they care about**: Operational efficiency, process standardization, supply chain reliability, business continuity, and getting high-impact individual contributors into seats fast.

**Pain points**:
- Operations roles are often undervalued in attracting top talent
- Supply chain disruptions require rapid organizational response and new talent
- Process improvement initiatives need Six Sigma / Lean professionals who are hard to source
- BAs and project managers are needed in surges (digital transformation, ERP implementations)

**How to approach**:
- Lead with specific sub-function expertise (supply chain vs. operations vs. BA vs. project management)
- Emphasize speed-to-productivity — contract placements that can contribute from week one
- Frame ERP/digital transformation staffing as a specialty (SAP, Oracle, Salesforce implementation consultants)

## Compliance Notes (Professional Services)

**Finance & Accounting**:
- CPA license verification: check with relevant state board of accountancy
- For SEC-adjacent roles (public company finance): ensure candidates understand Sarbanes-Oxley (SOX) compliance
- Background check: standard 7-year criminal + employment + credit check for fiduciary roles (controller, CFO)

**HR Professionals**:
- PHR/SPHR/SHRM-CP/SHRM-SCP certifications: verify with HRCI or SHRM registry
- For TA roles in healthcare/defense: may need to verify they understand clearance/compliance hiring processes

**General Professional Services**:
- Non-compete review: advise clients to confirm any candidate non-competes before extending an offer (HIS is not legal counsel — flag and refer)
- Reference checks: 2–3 professional references; for senior roles, at least one should be a former direct supervisor

## Executive Search Protocol

For Director, VP, and C-suite placements:

1. **Confidential intake**: 30-minute briefing with the CHRO or CEO; understand cultural fit, leadership style, not just functional requirements
2. **Candidate mapping**: Build a target list of 30–50 profiles from competitor companies, adjacent industries, and passive candidates
3. **Outreach**: Warm-channel approach (LinkedIn, referrals) — not a job board blast; confidentiality maintained
4. **Slate delivery**: Present 3–5 fully vetted, interested candidates within 45–60 days
5. **Interview coordination**: HIS manages scheduling, debrief capture, and offer negotiation support
6. **Guarantee**: 90-day replacement guarantee on C-suite; 60-day on Director/VP

**Fee**: 22–30% of first-year base salary (see `references/proposals-rates.md`)

## Market Context (Professional Services)

**Finance/Accounting**:
- CPA shortage is acute — new CPA completions are down ~30% vs. 10 years ago; experienced finance pros command premium
- Remote/hybrid preference is strong in finance — candidates expect flexibility
- Interim CFOs and controllers are increasingly common as companies defer permanent hires

**HR**:
- Post-layoff HR functions are being rebuilt (2023–2025 trend); HRBPs and TA specialists in demand
- HRIS skills (Workday especially) carry a significant premium over HRIS-agnostic HR roles
- DEI program builders are sought after but often misaligned to budget realities — set expectations

**Marketing**:
- Marketing budgets tightening → contract marketing over permanent is up
- Performance marketers (paid, analytics-driven) are in higher demand than brand marketers
- AI-adjacent marketing roles (prompt engineering for marketing, AI content strategy) are emerging

**Operations/Supply Chain**:
- Post-pandemic supply chain restructuring created lasting demand for supply chain managers
- ERP implementation projects (SAP S/4HANA migrations especially) driving consultant/specialist demand
- Project managers with PMP and Agile certs are consistently in shortage
