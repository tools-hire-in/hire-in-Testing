## Buyer Personas

### Chief Nursing Officer (CNO) / VP of Nursing

**What they care about**: Patient-to-nurse ratios, quality of care metrics, staff retention, compliance with credentialing standards, reducing agency dependence over time.

**Pain points**:
- Last-minute call-outs leaving floors short-staffed
- Credential packets that are incomplete and delay onboarding
- High travel nurse spend with poor retention
- CMS star ratings tied to staffing adequacy

**How to approach**:
- Lead with compliance and credential completeness — CredentialRx.ai is your primary differentiator
- Mention Joint Commission alignment and TJC-HCSS standards explicitly
- Offer to show the credential packet format before any placement — they've been burned before

**Sample opener**:
> "We know CNOs are tired of agencies who send candidates with incomplete credential packets. Every candidate Hire'in places arrives with a compliant submission packet — license verified, exclusion checks run, background cleared. Would a 15-minute walkthrough of our CredentialRx.ai process be useful?"

### Director of Human Resources / HR Business Partner (Healthcare)

**What they care about**: Vendor relationships, time-to-fill, cost control, compliance documentation, workforce planning.

**Pain points**:
- Too many staffing vendors with inconsistent quality
- Onboarding delays caused by missing documents
- Budget pressure to reduce per-diem and travel spend
- Managing multiple MSPs and credentialing timelines simultaneously

**How to approach**:
- Position HIS as a quality-over-volume partner — fewer submissions, better fit
- Show the compliance automation reduces HR admin burden
- Offer a consolidated reporting view on placements if they manage multiple agencies

---

### Staffing Coordinator / Scheduling Manager

**What they care about**: Fill rate, shift coverage, reliability of placed staff, speed of replacement when a contractor no-shows.

**Pain points**:
- Last-minute gaps that can't be filled fast enough
- Contractors who don't show up or leave mid-contract
- Limited visibility into upcoming coverage gaps

**How to approach**:
- Emphasize the HIS bench — pre-credentialed, available immediately
- Offer to set up a recurring "availability call" each week to stay ahead of gaps
- Guarantee replacement timeline when a placed contractor is unable to complete an assignment

## Compliance & Credentialing — Critical Notes

Healthcare is the most compliance-intensive domain. Know these before every client conversation:

**What CredentialRx.ai (proKred.com) does:**
- Automated license status checks across all 50-state public directories
- OIG exclusion list checks (required for Medicare/Medicaid-billing facilities)
- SAM.gov debarment check
- Compliant submission packet compilation (license copies, certifications, background reports, skills checklists)
- HIPAA-ready credential sharing via secure links

**What HIS verifies for every healthcare placement:**
- Active state nursing/clinical license (state-specific)
- BLS certification (required for all clinical roles)
- Additional certs as role requires: ACLS, PALS, NRP, TNCC, etc.
- SSN trace + 7-year criminal background check
- Employment verification (at minimum last 2 employers)
- Reference checks (2 professional, supervisor-preferred)
- TB test / health screening (facility-specific; HIS facilitates, facility specifies)
- Drug screen (facility-specific panel)

**Joint Commission (TJC) alignment:**
- HIS uses gold-standard skills checklists aligned to TJC HCSS (Healthcare Staffing Services) certification requirements
- Competency documentation available for all placed staff
- Can provide TJC-ready staffing documentation packets on request

**HIPAA compliance:**
- CredentialRx.ai is HIPAA-ready — PHI transmitted only through secure, encrypted channels
- No candidate health information stored in third-party CRMs

## Healthcare Rate Context

Refer to `references/proposals-rates.md` for full rate card. Healthcare-specific notes:
- Travel nurse rates have normalized post-pandemic but remain above pre-2020 levels; local contract is now 15–25% cheaper
- ICU/OR/ED specialty nurses command a 20–30% premium over Med/Surg rates
- Per-diem rates (same-day call) carry a 15–20% premium over contract rates
- Allied health rates vary significantly by specialty: OT/PT mid-tier; RT/rad tech closer to RN rates
- Physician locum tenens: quoted as daily rate, not hourly — ranges $1,500–$3,500+/day by specialty
- FQHC and rural health centers: often federally funded and rate-sensitive; offer volume commitment for rate flexibility
