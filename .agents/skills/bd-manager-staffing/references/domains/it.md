## Buyer Personas

### VP / Director of Engineering

**What they care about**: Team velocity, code quality, reducing interview burden, filling hard niche roles (cloud architects, ML engineers), and minimizing time-to-productivity.

**Pain points**:
- Too many unqualified resumes from generalist agencies
- Candidates who look good on paper but fail technical screens
- Roles open 60+ days draining team morale
- Agencies who don't understand the tech stack

**How to approach**:
- Lead with tech specificity: name the stack, the role type, the seniority level
- Mention KleriQ.ai's JD-to-sourcing logic — it means you speak their language
- Offer to have a recruiter who understands the role brief them, not just an account manager

**Sample opener**:
> "I noticed you've had a Senior Kubernetes/DevOps Engineer posting open for 8 weeks. Our team has placed 4 profiles in that specific niche in the last quarter — AI pre-screened, 24-hour delivery. Worth a 15-minute call?"

---

### CTO / Head of Technology

**What they care about**: Architectural decisions, build vs. buy talent strategy, keeping IP protected when using contractors, scaling faster than the competition.

**Pain points**:
- Founder-mode CTOs who do their own hiring — need to be convinced to delegate
- Fear of IP leakage with contractors
- Misalignment between business vision and engineering reality
- Pressure from investors on hiring milestones

**How to approach**:
- Position HIS as a technology-forward partner, not a body shop
- Emphasize KleriQ.ai as an AI system built by engineers, for engineering hiring
- Offer contractor IP protection frameworks and compliant SOWs as a value-add

---

### Director / Head of Talent Acquisition (TA)

**What they care about**: Reducing time-to-fill, improving hiring manager satisfaction, vendor management, reducing sourcing burden.

**Pain points**:
- Too many vendor relationships with inconsistent results
- Hiring managers going around TA to use agencies directly
- Sourcing niche IT talent they don't have deep domain expertise in
- ATS is full of unqualified resumes they never want to see

**How to approach**:
- Position HIS as a specialist extension of TA, not a replacement
- Emphasize that HIS submissions are pre-scored and pre-filtered — less noise
- Offer to set up a preferred workflow that fits into their existing ATS/VMS

## Compliance Notes (IT)

- **I-9 / E-Verify**: Every contractor placement includes I-9 verification and E-Verify enrollment
- **Background checks**: Standard 7-year criminal + employment verification; SSN trace; can extend to credit check for fintech/banking IT roles
- **IP / NDA**: HIS can provide standard contractor NDA templates; client's legal should finalize
- **FedRAMP / Clearance**: For federal IT roles, flag security clearance requirement early — cleared candidates require 2–3× longer sourcing lead time
- **W-2 vs. 1099**: HIS places on W-2 basis for contract roles; if client insists on 1099/C2C, clarify co-employment risk exposure

## IT Rate Context

Refer to `references/proposals-rates.md` for full rate card. IT-specific notes:
- AI/ML and cloud architect roles command a 15–25% premium over standard software engineering rates
- Cybersecurity (especially cleared) can exceed $200/hr for senior contractors
- QA/test automation roles are often 20–30% below SE rates — manage client expectations on both ends
- Location premium: San Francisco, New York, Seattle, Boston = highest; mid-market cities = 10–20% below
