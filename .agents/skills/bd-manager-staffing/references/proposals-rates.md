- One-sentence value proposition: *"Hire'in Solutions will deliver [role type] candidates within 24 hours, pre-vetted by AI and compliance-cleared, so [Company] can [business outcome]."*

- Proof metrics table (use the full metrics from SKILL.md)

### Section 3 — Our Technology Advantage
**KleriQ.ai** — describe as: "Proprietary recruiter intelligence engine that transforms complex job descriptions into sourcing logic, structured intake questions, and candidate scoring. This means every submission we make is pre-scored against your JD before it reaches your inbox."

**CredentialRx.ai** (healthcare only) — "Purpose-built compliance packet builder. Automated license and exclusion checks against all 50-state public directories, compliant submission packets, and HIPAA-ready credential sharing. Your candidates arrive credential-complete."

### Section 4 — Delivery Model
Describe the 4-step process:
1. **Submit Requirements** — detailed intake (30 min call or written brief); JD fed into KleriQ.ai
2. **AI Pre-screening** — KleriQ.ai scores candidates; eliminates 80%+ of unqualified resumes automatically
3. **Human Validation** — recruiter interviews for culture fit, communication, and EQ
4. **Delivery** — first submissions within 24 hours; full compliance documentation included

- All 50 US states coverage

- 24-hour first submission SLA
- AI + human validation means fewer bad hires

## Rate Card Frameworks

### Principles
- Always give a **range**, not a single number — markets fluctuate
- Explain what drives rate variation (seniority, location, stack/specialty, urgency)
- Never anchor too low — it signals desperation and devalues the service
- If pressed for a single number, give the midpoint and say "subject to job order details"

### Contract Staffing (Hourly Bill Rate)

Markup on contractor pay rate typically runs **45–65%** for professional roles. Bill rate = Pay rate × (1 + markup %).

| Domain | Typical Bill Rate Range (US) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| IT — junior (1–3 yrs) | $65–$90/hr | Frontend, QA, data analyst |
| IT — mid (3–7 yrs) | $90–$130/hr | Full-stack, DevOps, cloud |
| IT — senior/architect | $130–$200+/hr | Cloud architects, principal engineers |
| Healthcare — RN/LPN | $55–$95/hr | Varies by specialty and geography |
| Healthcare — Allied Health | $45–$80/hr | PT, OT, respiratory |
| Healthcare — Physician | $120–$250+/hr | Primary care vs. specialist |
| Engineering — mid | $75–$110/hr | Mechanical, civil, electrical |
| Engineering — senior | $110–$160/hr | Project managers, PEs |
| Professional Services | $65–$100/hr | Finance, HR, marketing |

### Contract-to-Hire

Typically: contract billing rate for trial period (3–6 months), then a **conversion fee of 10–15%** of first-year salary if the client converts to permanent.

> "We protect both sides: if they're not the right fit by month 3, we replace without an additional search fee."

### Permanent Placement (Direct Hire)

Fee: **15–25% of first-year base salary**, depending on seniority and domain.

| Seniority | Fee Range |
|---|---|
| Individual contributor | 15–18% |
| Manager/lead | 18–22% |
| Director/VP | 22–25% |
| C-suite / Executive | 25–30% (negotiable) |

**Guarantee period**: Offer 60–90 day replacement guarantee as standard. Healthcare roles may extend to 120 days given credentialing timelines.

### MSP/VMS Pricing Note
When working through an MSP or VMS, margins compress — expect 35–45% markup instead of 45–65%. This is normal; volume makes up for tighter margin. Flag this to the client if they're comparing direct vs. VMS proposals.

## Proposal Formatting Tips

- **Length**: 4–8 pages for a mid-market proposal; 2–3 pages for a small business
- **Tone**: Consultative, not salesy. Mirror the client's industry language.
- **Visuals**: Use Hire'in brand colors: Navy #1F3A6E and Orange #F47C20
- **Attach**: One relevant case study as a separate appendix, not embedded in the main doc
- **Personalize**: The cover page and executive summary must mention the client by name and reference their specific role/challenge
