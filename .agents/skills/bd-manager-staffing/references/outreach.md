# Outreach Playbook — Hire'in Solutions

## Core Outreach Principles

1. **One pain, one ask** — every message addresses a single business pain and ends with one clear next step.
2. **Personalize the first line** — mention a company trigger (job posting volume, funding, expansion, news) before any pitch.
3. **Lead with outcomes, not services** — say "25 ICU nurses placed in 21 days" not "we do healthcare staffing."
4. **Short is always better** — decision-makers delete long emails. 150 words is the ceiling for cold outreach.
5. **Always include a PS** — the PS is the second-most-read part of a cold email.

## Multi-Touch Follow-Up Sequence

### Timing & Channel Guide

| Touch | Day | Channel | Goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Day 0 | Email (cold) | Introduce + one ask |
| 2 | Day 3 | LinkedIn connection | Warm the name |
| 3 | Day 5 | Email (value add) | Share insight or case study |
| 4 | Day 10 | LinkedIn InMail | Softer check-in |
| 5 | Day 15 | Phone call | Live conversation attempt |
| 6 | Day 25 | Email (breakup) | Give them an easy out |

### Touch 3 — Value-Add Email

**Subject:** [Domain] hiring insight — thought this might be relevant

```
Hi [Name],

Didn't want to just follow up without bringing something useful.

[One specific market insight relevant to their domain — e.g., "RN travel pay rates have dropped 18% since 2023, making per-diem staffing more viable again" or "We're seeing a 40% increase in contract-to-hire IT requests as companies test talent before committing."]

Hire'in Solutions tracks these trends closely — happy to share what we're seeing in your specific market if useful.

[Name]
```

### Touch 6 — Breakup Email

**Subject:** Should I stop reaching out?

```
Hi [Name],

I've reached out a few times and haven't heard back — totally understandable, you're busy.

I'll take this as a sign the timing isn't right. I'll leave you with one thing: if you ever need [domain] talent placed fast and correctly, hire-in.com is where to start.

I'll step back for now, but feel free to reach out anytime.

[Name] | Hire'in Solutions
```

## Personalization Rules by Trigger Signal

| Signal | Personalization angle |
|---|---|
| 5+ open roles in same function | "Scaling fast — we can compress that timeline" |
| Recent funding round | "Growth mode means needing the right talent immediately" |
| New office/expansion news | "Expansion means new hiring markets — we cover all 50 states" |
| High Glassdoor turnover signals | "High turnover is often a fit problem — our AI pre-screens for culture alignment too" |
| MSP/VMS program mentioned | Use the MSP objection script from `references/call-prep.md` |
