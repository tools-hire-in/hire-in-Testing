For a Talent Acquisition / Staffing company covering the entire United States, I would not keep one single shift for everyone. I would design fixed core shifts in the system, but allow client/account-specific flexibility with manager approval.

The main coverage goal should be:

U.S. business coverage from 8:00 AM Eastern to 6:00 PM Pacific
That means your company is effectively covering from 5:00 AM PT to 6:00 PM PT across the day.

Company Policy Language You Can Use

Core Shift Policy

Each employee will be assigned a fixed core working shift in the company attendance and HR system based on their role, department, client coverage responsibility, and business need. The core shift represents the employee’s official availability, attendance, collaboration, and productivity window.

Client-facing work may require reasonable flexibility outside the assigned core shift based on time zone, candidate availability, urgent submissions, interviews, onboarding, compliance deadlines, or account-specific needs. Any regular shift adjustment must be approved by the reporting manager and updated in the employee profile.

Failure to maintain an assigned core shift in the system may result in attendance, productivity, compliance, and payroll-related discrepancies. Employees are responsible for ensuring their availability, communication status, and attendance are aligned with their approved shift.

My Recommendation

For your company, I would use three official core shifts only to keep it clean:

Early U.S. Shift: 5:30 PM – 2:30 AM IST
Standard U.S. Shift: 8:00 PM – 5:00 AM IST
Late U.S. Shift: 9:30 PM – 6:30 AM IST

Do not create too many shift types. Too many shifts will create confusion in attendance, payroll, and performance tracking.

For most recruiters, keep 8:00 PM – 5:00 AM IST as the standard.
For healthcare/state accounts, assign early coverage.
For West Coast clients and late candidate calls, assign late coverage.
