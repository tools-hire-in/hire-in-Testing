Do not allow open free-text by default.

Instead use:

Pre-approved templates:

Each template should have sentence blocks mapped to dropdown selections.

Important caution

I would not include:

Bad
Poor
Weak
Unsatisfactory

in outward-facing experience letters.

That is the wrong design.

Better rule

For people with poor performance, the system should still generate:

a neutral factual letter
with no praise beyond minimum professional wording

So instead of “bad,” use:

Basic / Standard / Factual only
Why

Experience letters in India are usually used for:

next employer
background verification
visa/university/admin use

Negative wording creates unnecessary dispute and HR risk. For difficult exits, issue a factual version, not a negative version.

Best structure for your feature
Mode A: Factual only

For sensitive or poor-performance exits

Generated line example:

“During the above tenure, the employee was associated with the organization in the role of [Designation].”
“This letter is issued upon request for record purposes.”
Mode B: Standard positive

For normal exits

Generated line example:

“During the tenure, the employee carried out assigned responsibilities in the [Department] function and maintained satisfactory professional conduct.”
Mode C: Strong positive

For good performers

Generated line example:

“During the tenure, the employee demonstrated diligence, professionalism, and a positive contribution to assigned responsibilities.”
Mode D: Excellent

For top performers

Generated line example:

“During the tenure, the employee demonstrated a high level of professionalism, ownership, and effectiveness in assigned responsibilities.”

My BA recommendation

Build this in Phase 1 or early Phase 2 of the HR module, because:

very visible HR value

Compared to many other HR features, this one has a very good effort-to-value ratio.

Final recommendation

Yes, build it.

But build it as a standardized certificate engine, not a custom writing workflow.

Best model
HR enters only core person data and dropdown selections
wording is fully controlled by policy
bad/poor wording should not be exposed
poor performers get factual-only letters
strong performers get stronger approved wording
admin override exists only for rare cases with audit trail

That is the most professional, scalable, and legally safer design.
