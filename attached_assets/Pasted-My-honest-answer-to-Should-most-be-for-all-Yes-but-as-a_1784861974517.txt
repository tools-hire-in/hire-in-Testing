My honest answer to “Should most be for all?”
Yes — but as awareness, not full certification.

I would make these mandatory awareness for ALL:

SOP	Why all should know it
GOV-001	Everyone needs to know SOPs are controlled and versioned
REQ-001	Everyone should understand Hire’in’s clarity-first model
TA-001	Everyone should know no work starts without intake/rate clarity
TA-003	Submission quality is a company standard, not only recruiter work
CX-001	Candidate experience is brand-critical
PLAT-001	Everyone must understand KleriQ/ProKred are separate SaaS platforms
LEGAL-001	Everyone handles some form of confidential or sensitive data
OPS-001	Tool access and security affects all users
HR-001	Everyone must understand training, acknowledgment, and performance linkage
AUDIT-001	Everyone should know KPIs and audits are part of operations
TRAIN-001	Everyone should know how training and certification work

That is 11 out of 21 SOPs as ALL awareness.

But I would not make every employee complete deep training for Finance, BD, Marketing, Healthcare Credentialing, IT Technical Screening, Engineering/Professional Services, and Manager Templates unless it applies to their role.

Final recommendation

Your instinct is correct:

Most SOPs should be visible to everyone.
Many SOPs should have awareness training for everyone.
Only role-relevant SOPs should require certification.

That gives you the best balance:

Common company language without training overload.
Role accountability without confusion.
Compliance evidence without fake checkbox completion.
