2. COMPANYWIDE POLICIES AND COMMUNICATION STANDARDS
Companywide Policies

Please note and start following these strictly going forward.

2.1 Pay Rate and Requisition Discussions

If you have any concerns about pay rates or open requisitions, bring them up in the designated work discussion channel.

Policy

Do not keep pay or requirement concerns limited to private silos

Avoid one-on-one side discussions unless specifically required

Bring relevant concerns to the approved team discussion area

Open discussion creates alignment and prevents confusion

Escalate blockers early instead of delaying action

2.2 Remote Team Communication

Since we are a remote team, clear and transparent communication is essential.

Policy

It is better to slightly over-communicate than to miss critical details

Important work updates must be visible to relevant stakeholders

Assumptions should be minimized

Silence causes delays, confusion, and missed accountability

2.3 Official Work Communication Platform

No work communication should happen on WhatsApp, personal texts, or personal chat channels.

Policy

Microsoft Teams is the official company work platform

All work discussions, updates, clarifications, and decisions must happen in Teams

Personal messaging channels must not be used for work operations

Documentation and visibility are mandatory

2.4 Microsoft Teams Etiquette

All team members must be active and consistently available on MS Teams during working hours.

Mandatory expectations

Be present and engaged during working hours

Acknowledge relevant messages with a reaction or brief reply

Silence is not acceptable when awareness is expected

Use threads to keep conversations organized

Keep communication professional, concise, and respectful

Share documents and updates in Teams for visibility

Update Teams status when away or unavailable

2.5 Professional Conduct

Every employee is expected to maintain a professional standard in all interactions.

Expectations

be respectful

be clear and factual

avoid blame-driven communication

focus on resolution and ownership

do not overpromise to candidates or clients

do not misrepresent rates, roles, or next steps

2.6 Documentation Discipline

If work is not documented, it is treated as incomplete.

Expectations

candidate interactions must be documented

important updates must be visible

recruiter notes must be useful and factual

status fields must be updated properly

another team member should be able to review your work without needing verbal explanation

2.7 Responsiveness

Prompt acknowledgment and timely response are required.

Expectations

acknowledge internal messages promptly

respond to urgent candidate/client items quickly

inform the team early if a delay or blocker arises

do not allow critical communication to go unanswered

2.8 Attendance and Availability

Employees must maintain proper workday discipline.

Expectations

follow assigned working hours

accurately reflect attendance if a portal/system is used

record check-in and check-out properly when required

update availability status correctly to avoid reporting or payroll issues

2.9 Confidentiality

Candidate and client information is confidential and must be handled carefully.

Expectations

do not share resumes or contact details outside approved channels

do not circulate pay rates or client information casually

use only company-approved systems and storage

maintain confidentiality in all communications

2.10 Policy Violations

Failure to follow communication, documentation, responsiveness, or process standards may lead to performance action.

3. HEALTHCARE RECRUITMENT SOP AND DEFINITION OF DONE
Objective

To ensure every healthcare candidate submission is accurate, timely, compliant, and aligned to the client requirement.

Why healthcare recruiting requires extra discipline

Healthcare recruiting is highly sensitive because errors in specialty match, credential review, shift alignment, or compliance can quickly damage trust with the client and waste critical time.

Step 1: Requisition Intake
Review the following

client or facility name

role title

specialty

location

shift

duration

bill rate or pay guidance

years of experience required

mandatory certifications

active license requirements

EMR or specialty system experience

start date

submission deadline

Definition of Done

A requisition is intake-complete only when:

role title and specialty are clear

location and shift are understood

required license/certifications are identified

compensation guidance is understood or escalated

submission timeline is known

must-have criteria are clear

Step 2: Sourcing
Source from

internal database

job boards

prior redeployable candidates

referrals

healthcare specialty talent pools

previous pipelines

Definition of Done

Sourcing is complete for the current cycle when:

a reasonable number of relevant candidates have been identified

outreach has started

candidate pool is segmented by quality and readiness

sourcing effort is documented

Step 3: Candidate Screening
Validate the following

specialty alignment

active license status

required certifications

recent relevant experience

shift fit

location fit

start availability

travel readiness if applicable

compensation expectations

onboarding or compliance blockers

Definition of Done

A healthcare screen is complete only when:

major compliance items are checked

availability is confirmed

candidate interest is confirmed

pay expectations are discussed

recruiter notes are entered clearly

Step 4: Submission Preparation
Submission should include

updated resume

candidate fit summary

license details

certification details

availability

compensation expectations if process requires

recruiter notes

known risks or limitations

Definition of Done

Submission is complete only when:

all client-required fields are complete

resume is current and clean

specialty fit is explained properly

compliance-related details are not missing

notes are documented accurately

Step 5: Interview Coordination
Recruiter responsibilities

confirm interview availability

prepare candidate on role, unit, and client context

reconfirm interest before interview

collect immediate post-interview feedback

Definition of Done

Interview stage is complete when:

interview is confirmed

candidate is prepared

feedback is collected

next steps are documented

Step 6: Offer and Pre-Onboarding
Verify

candidate acceptance terms

joining or start timeline

document readiness

credentialing readiness

location and travel logistics if applicable

Definition of Done

Offer stage is complete when:

verbal acceptance is confirmed

next documentation step is initiated

start-date risks are assessed

follow-up ownership is clear

Healthcare red flags

unclear or inactive license status

specialty mismatch

inconsistent experience details

unstable compensation expectations

slow or unreliable communication

inability to start within required window

hidden location or shift restrictions

Healthcare submission quality rules

Do not submit candidates:

with unclear license status

with poor specialty alignment

without discussing pay expectations

without confirming availability

without proper recruiter notes

without reviewing basic compliance items

4. IT RECRUITMENT SOP AND DEFINITION OF DONE
Objective

To ensure every IT candidate submission is technically aligned, properly screened, well-documented, and ready for client review.

Why IT recruiting needs strong requirement interpretation

In IT recruiting, weak interpretation of must-have skills leads to poor submissions, lost client confidence, and wasted time. Recruiters must understand role depth, not just keywords.

Step 1: Requisition Intake
Review the following

client name

role title

contract, CTH, or full-time nature

location or remote expectation

required tech stack

nice-to-have skills

years of experience

domain or industry preference

interview process

salary or rate range

work authorization constraints

urgency and timeline

Definition of Done

Intake is complete only when:

must-have and nice-to-have skills are separated

work model is understood

compensation is clear or escalated

visa/work authorization expectations are known

target profile is understood

Step 2: Sourcing
Focus areas

exact skill match

adjacent transferable experience

recent project relevance

location and work model fit

availability to interview and join

Definition of Done

Sourcing is complete for the current cycle when:

enough relevant candidates have been targeted

sourcing channels are documented

candidates are ranked by fit and readiness

Step 3: Technical Screening
Validate the following

hands-on technical skills

years of recent experience

current or recent project relevance

tools/platform depth

communication quality

reason for move

salary/rate expectations

notice period

work authorization

location or remote alignment

Definition of Done

A technical screen is complete only when:

must-have skill match is validated

recent hands-on depth is discussed

candidate interest is genuine

compensation is discussed

work authorization is confirmed

notes explain both fit and risk

Step 4: Submission Preparation
Submission should include

updated resume

recruiter summary

must-have skill match notes

availability

salary/rate expectations if needed

work authorization

interview constraints

location or relocation constraints if relevant

Definition of Done

Submission is complete only when:

recruiter summary clearly explains fit

must-have alignment is addressed

availability is confirmed

work authorization is captured

resume quality is acceptable

major risks are noted

Step 5: Interview Coordination
Recruiter responsibilities

confirm candidate timing

prepare the candidate on client, role, and interview expectations

ensure candidate represents skill depth honestly

gather feedback immediately after interview

Definition of Done

Interview stage is complete when:

interview is confirmed

candidate is prepared

recruiter captures feedback

next action is documented

Step 6: Offer and Close
Verify

compensation agreement

notice period

counteroffer risk

joining date

documentation readiness

Definition of Done

Offer stage is complete when:

verbal acceptance is confirmed

joining risks are identified

follow-up cadence is defined

candidate control is maintained until joining

IT red flags

weak must-have skill match

inconsistent technical claims

inability to explain recent projects

unstable compensation expectations

location mismatch

unclear work authorization

poor communication quality

IT submission quality rules

Do not submit candidates:

based only on keywords without proper validation

with weak must-have alignment

without discussing compensation

without confirming work authorization

without a recruiter summary

without documenting risks

5. RECRUITER COMMUNICATION TEMPLATES AND PLAYBOOK
5.1 Initial Candidate Outreach

Hello [Candidate Name],
I hope you are doing well. I came across your profile and wanted to connect regarding a [Job Title] opportunity with [Client/Industry]. Based on your experience in [Skill/Domain], I believe this could be relevant. Please let me know if you would be open to a quick discussion.

5.2 Follow-Up Outreach

Hello [Candidate Name],
Following up on my earlier note regarding the [Job Title] opportunity. This role looks relevant to your background, and I would be happy to share more details if you are open to exploring it.

5.3 Screening Invite

Hello [Candidate Name],
Thank you for your interest. I would like to schedule a short screening discussion to understand your recent experience, availability, compensation expectations, and fit for the role. Please share a few suitable time slots.

5.4 Candidate Submission Confirmation

Hello [Candidate Name],
Thank you for speaking with me. Based on our discussion, I am moving forward with your profile for the [Job Title] role. Please let me know immediately if anything changes regarding your availability, interest, interview schedule, compensation, or ongoing opportunities.

5.5 Interview Confirmation

Hello [Candidate Name],
Your interview has been scheduled for [Date/Time]. Please be available a little early and ensure you are in a quiet setting. I am sharing the interview details and preparation notes separately.

5.6 Post-Interview Feedback Request

Hello [Candidate Name],
Thank you for attending the interview. Please share your feedback as soon as possible, including how the discussion went, your interest level, and any concerns you may have.

5.7 Offer Discussion

Hello [Candidate Name],
I am pleased to inform you that the client would like to proceed further. Before we move ahead, please reconfirm your acceptance parameters, joining timeline, compensation understanding, and any risks or pending interviews.

5.8 Rejection Message

Hello [Candidate Name],
Thank you for your time and interest. At this stage, the client has decided not to move forward with the application. I appreciate your time and would be glad to stay in touch regarding future opportunities that align with your profile.

5.9 Internal Clarification Template

Hi Team,
Need clarification on the following for requisition [Req ID / Role]:

rate or pay question:

must-have skill question:

submission requirement question:

timeline concern:
Please advise so we can move ahead correctly.

Communication rules for recruiters

keep communication professional and concise

do not overpromise

do not misrepresent the role

do not hide constraints

do not pressure candidates unreasonably

document all important communication

follow up with discipline, not spam
