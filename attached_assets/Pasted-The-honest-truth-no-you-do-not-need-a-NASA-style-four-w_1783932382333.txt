The one missing capability that matters most

Your system already has reminders and automations.

The biggest likely missing capability is:

Action ownership and verified closure.

A reminder says:

Your check-in is due.

Governance says:

The manager check-in was due, the reminder was sent, the manager did not complete it, the employee has an unresolved blocker, the skip-level manager has now been assigned an action, and the issue remains open.

That is the real governance layer.

Recommended practical sequence

2. Confirm hierarchy and privacy
3. Add shared action/completion/closure tracking
4. Connect Goals and Check-ins

Final recommendation

Stop expanding the audit program.

Use a small implementation-readiness review, then build a focused governance MVP around the processes that already exist.

The Hire’in Governance Layer should simply ensure:

Every important requirement has an owner, a deadline, evidence, manager oversight, escalation and verified closure—and the CEO sees only material exceptions.

That will create real execution discipline without building a system more complicated than the company it is meant to manage.
