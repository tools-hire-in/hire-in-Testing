A very useful line for the meeting

You can say:

> “We see our role primarily as the healthcare recruiting and delivery engine. For downstream items like compliance, credentialing, and payroll, we’d want to plug into your existing process rather than duplicate it.”

That line is strong because:

it sets scope

sounds collaborative

avoids sounding unwilling

reinforces specialization

On the “bench” question

Yes — still avoid “bench.”

Say:

> “For healthcare, we typically refer to this as active pipeline rather than bench. We can show current candidate flow and pipeline depth by role and geography.”

That is exactly the right correction without sounding defensive.

My final recommendation

Tomorrow, your presentation should aim to communicate:

“We can become your healthcare recruitment delivery arm, without requiring you to build an internal healthcare team.”

Not:

“We can take over every downstream staffing function.”

That distinction matters a lot.
