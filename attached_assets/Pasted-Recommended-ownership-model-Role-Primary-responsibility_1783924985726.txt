The critical policy distinction

You should establish this policy:

Work performed but not recorded in CEIPAL within the required SLA is not considered verified staffing activity.

But the Governance Layer must still distinguish between:

Not evidenced

No CEIPAL record supports the claimed work.

Process noncompliance

The employee completed work but failed to update CEIPAL within the required timeframe.

Performance variance

The required business activity or outcome was not completed.

System or integration failure

CEIPAL was unavailable, the integration failed or the record could not be saved.

Manager support failure

The employee raised a blocker but the manager did not respond or correct it.

These cannot be treated as the same problem.

For example:

CEIPAL shows four submissions against a target of six.

The system must not immediately say:

Recruiter failed to perform.

It should establish:

Were six submissions actually expected based on active demand?
Are all submissions recorded?
Was CEIPAL available?
Were assigned requirements complete?
Was the employee waiting for manager or client clarification?
Did the manager validate the discrepancy?
Were there approved exceptions?
