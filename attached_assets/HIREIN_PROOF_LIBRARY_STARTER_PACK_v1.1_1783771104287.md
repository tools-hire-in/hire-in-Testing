# Approval Rule

A card becomes **Approved** only when:

- The evidence is confirmed as current
- The permitted wording is accepted
- The restrictions are accepted
- An authorized owner enters their name and approval date

Use this sign-off format:

```text
Approval status: Approved
Approved by: [Name]
Approval date: [YYYY-MM-DD]
```

Until signed, keep the status as:

```text
Approval status: Ready for approval
```

# IT Staffing and KlerHire Extension

# Evidence Integrity Rule

These cards are drafted from three evidence levels:

1. **Document-supported:** Existing material directly supports the narrow claim.
2. **Operational-sign-off required:** The claim is plausible and consistent with Hire’in’s operating model, but needs an accountable owner and internal records.
3. **Product-verification required:** KlerHire functionality must be demonstrated by the product owner before public use.

Do not approve a card merely because the wording sounds accurate.

A card is Approved only after:

- Evidence is attached or cited
- The evidence owner confirms the claim
- Restrictions are accepted
- Approval name and date are entered
