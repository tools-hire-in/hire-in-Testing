Best design:

Responsibilities summary = based on role + level
Performance wording = separate dropdown
Conduct wording = separate dropdown

That keeps the letter clean and consistent.

For experience letters, the responsibilities block should stay factual and role-based, not emotional. Then performance can add one controlled sentence like “performance was good” or “demonstrated strong professionalism.”

Recommended UI setup

For the field in your screenshot:

Dropdown label: Select responsibilities description

Best options source: auto-filter by selected designation
So if HR selects Healthcare Recruiter - Sr, the dropdown should only show summaries for that role.

Also, I would keep:

1 default summary per role-level
1 alternate summary per role-level only if you want variation
Copy-ready responsibility summaries
1) Healthcare Sourcer – Intern

Option A
Supported sourcing activities for healthcare positions by identifying potential candidates, screening basic profile fit, maintaining candidate records, and assisting the recruitment team with pipeline updates and follow-ups.

Option B
Assisted in talent sourcing for healthcare roles through profile review, candidate outreach support, database maintenance, and coordination of candidate information for the recruiting team.

2) Healthcare Sourcer – Senior

Option A
Handled sourcing for healthcare positions by building candidate pipelines, identifying qualified professionals, conducting initial profile assessments, maintaining sourcing trackers, and supporting timely submission readiness.

Option B
Managed targeted sourcing for healthcare requirements through market mapping, resume screening, candidate outreach, database organization, and ongoing pipeline support aligned with business priorities.

3) Healthcare Sourcer – Lead

Option A
Led healthcare sourcing activities by driving candidate pipeline generation, guiding sourcing priorities, supporting team coordination, maintaining sourcing quality standards, and enabling recruiters with qualified candidate flow.

Option B
Oversaw sourcing execution for healthcare roles through pipeline planning, profile quality checks, team guidance, sourcing process improvement, and support for timely recruitment delivery.

4) Healthcare Recruiter – Intern

Option A
Supported healthcare recruitment activities by assisting with candidate sourcing, screening coordination, interview scheduling, database updates, and follow-up communication throughout the hiring process.

Option B
Assisted the recruitment team in handling healthcare hiring tasks including candidate coordination, basic profile review, scheduling support, and maintaining recruitment records and status updates.

5) Healthcare Recruiter – Senior

Option A
Managed end-to-end recruitment for healthcare positions including candidate sourcing, screening, interview coordination, submission management, offer follow-up, and stakeholder communication.

Option B
Handled full-cycle healthcare recruitment by identifying suitable candidates, evaluating profile fit, coordinating interviews, maintaining candidate engagement, and supporting closure of open requirements.

6) Healthcare Recruiter – Lead

Option A
Led healthcare recruitment delivery by managing critical requirements, supporting team members, driving submission quality, coordinating hiring activities, and ensuring timely follow-up across the recruitment lifecycle.

Option B
Oversaw recruitment execution for healthcare roles through candidate pipeline management, screening oversight, recruiter guidance, process coordination, and focus on quality and turnaround timelines.

7) Healthcare Recruiter – Manager

Option A
Managed healthcare recruitment operations by overseeing team performance, monitoring hiring pipelines, supporting delivery planning, coordinating with stakeholders, and ensuring process discipline, quality, and target achievement.

Option B
Led the healthcare recruitment function through team supervision, workload planning, escalation handling, performance monitoring, and alignment of hiring delivery with business and client requirements.

8) IT Recruiter – Intern

Option A
Supported IT recruitment activities by assisting with candidate sourcing, basic profile screening, interview coordination, applicant tracking updates, and follow-up communication during the hiring process.

Option B
Assisted the recruitment team in IT hiring operations through profile review support, candidate coordination, scheduling assistance, tracker maintenance, and timely status updates.

9) IT Recruiter – Senior

Option A
Managed end-to-end recruitment for IT positions including sourcing, screening, candidate evaluation, interview coordination, submission handling, and offer-stage follow-up.

Option B
Handled full-cycle IT recruitment by identifying qualified candidates, assessing technical profile alignment, coordinating interviews, maintaining candidate engagement, and supporting hiring closures.

10) IT Recruiter – Lead

Option A
Led IT recruitment delivery by managing priority requirements, guiding recruiters, improving submission quality, coordinating hiring workflows, and ensuring timely progress across open positions.

Option B
Oversaw recruitment execution for IT roles through team support, candidate pipeline management, screening oversight, process improvement, and delivery alignment with hiring goals.

11) IT Recruiter – Manager

Option A
Managed IT recruitment operations by supervising team performance, planning hiring delivery, monitoring open positions, supporting stakeholder communication, and ensuring quality, compliance, and target achievement.

Option B
Led the IT recruitment function through team management, process oversight, escalation resolution, hiring pipeline monitoring, and alignment of recruitment outcomes with business needs.

Do this as a separate dropdown, not inside the responsibilities text.

Recommended performance values

Standard
Good

Excellent
Copy-ready performance lines

Factual Only
During the above tenure, the employee was associated with the organization in the stated role and department.

Standard
During the tenure, the employee carried out assigned responsibilities in a satisfactory manner.

Good
During the tenure, the employee demonstrated diligence and responsibility in assigned work.

Very Good
During the tenure, the employee demonstrated professionalism, commitment, and good execution of assigned responsibilities.

Excellent
During the tenure, the employee demonstrated a high level of professionalism, ownership, and effectiveness in assigned responsibilities.

auto-load these based on designation
allow HR to pick Option A or Option B
then select performance band
then generate PDF

That is cleaner, faster, and much more scalable.
