# Hire’in Proof Library Extraction Interview
## Launch Gate for the AI Content Agent

**Version:** 1.0  
**Purpose:** Convert real Hire’in operating evidence into approved PC-## proof cards that the content agent can use safely.

---

# 1. Launch Rule

Do not launch the upgraded content agent into routine production use until at least:

```text
10 approved proof cards
```

exist in `HIREIN_PROOF_LIBRARY.md`.

A card counts toward the minimum only when it includes:

- Exact claim
- Supporting evidence
- Evidence owner
- Permitted wording
- Restrictions
- Named-client permission where applicable
- Approval status
- Review or expiration date

A goal, projection, capability aspiration, draft deck statement, or undocumented memory is not proof.

---

# 2. Required Interview Participants

Include the people who can validate the facts:

- Founder/CEO
- IT delivery or sales leader
- Healthcare delivery leader
- Operations or compliance owner
- KlerHire/product owner
- Person with access to contracts, reports, client emails, placement records, or performance data

One person may cover multiple roles, but every approved claim must have a named accountable owner.

---

# 3. Evidence to Gather Before the Interview

Bring available:

- Contracts, MSAs, SOWs, purchase orders, and vendor approvals
- Invoices or placement records
- ATS/VMS reports
- Submission, interview, offer, and start records
- Client or partner emails
- Approved testimonials
- Capability statements
- Corporate formation and operating-history records
- KlerHire product documentation
- Security, compliance, certification, or registration evidence
- Public-sector contract or program documentation
- Screenshots or exports that can be retained internally
- Named-client usage permissions

Do not upload sensitive candidate PII into the marketing proof library.

---

# 4. Minimum Ten-Card Coverage

Create at least one approved card in each applicable category.

## PC-01 — Company operating history

Questions:

- What year did Hire’in begin operating?
- What legal or business records confirm it?
- Does the evidence support general staffing history, IT history, healthcare history, or all three?
- What exact public wording is safe?

## PC-02 — IT staffing capability

Questions:

- Which IT role families have actually been supported?
- What delivery models were used?
- What evidence exists?
- Can niche or critical-role language be supported?

## PC-03 — Healthcare staffing capability

Questions:

- Which healthcare role families have actually been supported?
- What client or program models were used?
- What evidence exists?
- Which credential-aware process claims are supportable?

## PC-04 — Requirement calibration and screening process

Questions:

- What intake steps are consistently used?
- What screening or quality-control artifacts exist?
- Who owns the process?
- Is this a documented process or an aspiration?

## PC-05 — Communication and transparency

Questions:

- What status cadence, tracker, ATS, VMS, or escalation process is actually used?
- What evidence demonstrates the practice?
- Which wording is accurate without claiming an outcome?

## PC-06 — MSP/VMS and partner delivery

Questions:

- Which MSP, VMS, subcontract, or partner workflows have been used?
- What can be named publicly?
- What submission, ownership, SLA, or reporting claims are supportable?

## PC-07 — KlerHire approved capabilities

Questions:

- What functions work today?
- What is production, beta, internal, or planned?
- What screenshots or product documentation support the claim?
- What wording avoids implying autonomous hiring decisions?

## PC-08 — Public-sector readiness or experience

Questions:

- Which state or federal programs, registrations, contracts, or vendor approvals are real and current?
- Which can be named?
- What must never be implied?
- Are any certifications or contract vehicles expired or restricted?

## PC-09 — Named-client or representative-experience claim

Questions:

- What was Hire’in’s exact relationship: direct vendor, subcontractor, sourcing partner, or downstream supplier?
- Was there a placement, submission, project, or only an introduction?
- Is public naming permitted?
- Is logo use permitted?
- What restrictions apply?

## PC-10 — Measurable result or case study

Questions:

- What real outcome can be supported?
- What is the measurement period?
- What is the source report?
- Is the result attributable to Hire’in?
- Can it be anonymized?
- What caveats are required?

# 6. Proof Card Template

Use the exact schema already defined in `HIREIN_PROOF_LIBRARY.md`.

At minimum:

```text
PC-##
Title:

Claim:
[Exact fact the evidence supports]

Evidence:
[Document, report, email, system record, contract, screenshot, or other source]

Evidence owner:
[Named person accountable for validation]

Evidence date:
[YYYY-MM-DD or applicable period]

Permitted wording:
[Exact public language the agent may use]

Restrictions:
[Anonymize / no logo / no client name / internal only / specific context only]

Named-client permission:
[Allowed / prohibited / not applicable]

Valid through:
[Date or review cadence]

Approval status:
[Draft / verified / approved / retired]

Approved by:
[Name and date]
```

---

# 7. Interview Discipline

For every proposed claim, ask:

1. What exactly happened?
2. What evidence proves it?
3. Who can verify it?
4. Is it current?
5. Can it be named publicly?
6. What is the narrowest accurate wording?
7. What would make this wording misleading?
8. When should the claim be reviewed again?

Reject vague answers such as:

- “We have done a lot of these.”
- “Everyone knows we support that.”
- “We could do it.”
- “The tool should be able to.”
- “The client will probably be fine with being named.”

---

# 8. Approval Outcomes

Each claim ends in one status:

## Approved

The agent may use the exact permitted wording.

## Approved with restrictions

The agent may use it only under the documented limits.

## Needs evidence

Keep it as `[NEEDS_PROOF]`.

## Not approved

Do not use it publicly.

## Retired

Previously valid, but no longer current or permitted.
