3. Resolve the repository-editing contradiction

A file stored at docs/bd-agent-playbook.md cannot also be freely edited by a nontechnical Knowledge Owner unless a controlled publishing mechanism is built.

For the MVP:

The Knowledge Owner owns and approves the content.

4. Keep operational guidance separate from factual evidence

The playbook may guide:

questions to ask
workflow steps
qualification logic
objection handling

approved communication structure

But the playbook itself must not become the evidence source for customer-facing claims.

For example, the playbook may say:

The actual statistic, client result, certification, coverage claim, or case-study result must still come from an approved Knowledge Library source with its own citation and claim status.

This prevents internally written sales instructions from accidentally becoming “proof.”

Product Documentation Decision

The Hire’in BD Agent will ship with one authoritative, version-controlled operating playbook located at:

docs/bd-agent-playbook.md

The playbook is a required product component, not optional supporting documentation.

It serves three purposes simultaneously:

Onboarding guide — enables a new BD representative to understand and use the system from the first day.
Operational reference — tells active users what to do next during a live opportunity.
Governance manual — defines how managers, Knowledge Owners, and Super Admins maintain quality, permissions, evidence, and approved content.

The playbook is rendered inside the BD Agent interface and is not distributed only as an email attachment or external PDF.

Canonical Source and Governance

The published Markdown file in the repository is the canonical source of truth.

The Knowledge Owner is accountable for the accuracy, completeness, and approval of its business content. Repository publication follows the platform's established change and review process.

For the MVP:

the playbook is rendered read-only inside the product;
direct browser-based editing is not required;
the Knowledge Owner reviews and approves proposed updates;
approved changes are committed to the repository;
each material update changes the playbook version and revision date.

The file must begin with the following governance metadata:

Playbook version
Product version supported
Content owner
Last approved date
Next scheduled review date
Approved domains
Change summary

the correct next action.

approving claims and proof points;

coaching actions for low-quality usage.

KPIs must distinguish between:

product adoption;
workflow quality;
pipeline influence;
revenue attribution.

Provides concise role-specific reference cards.

Knowledge Owner weekly governance card;

Contextual help must display the relevant portion of the playbook rather than loading the entire document into every screen.

The system should render or retrieve the relevant section using stable heading anchors. It must not send the entire playbook to the AI model on every request.

Evidence Boundary

The playbook is an operational and behavioral source.

It may guide workflow behavior, conversation strategy, messaging structure, qualification questions, objection handling, and recommended actions.

It must not independently authorize external factual claims.

Customer-facing claims concerning performance, clients, credentials, certifications, geographic coverage, compliance, delivery speed, results, savings, placements, or case studies must resolve to separately approved Knowledge Library evidence.
