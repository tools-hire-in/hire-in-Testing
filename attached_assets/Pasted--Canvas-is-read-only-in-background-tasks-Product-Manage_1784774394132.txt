Planning calendar display logic (16 seconds)
Great inputs — let me give you my honest BA take on each, then revise the final design and lay out the task sequence in plain English.

✅ Q1 — Should Calendar show both Articles and Social Posts?
My honest take: Yes, and it's actually the right call.

The Calendar should be a true Editorial Calendar — the "air traffic control" view of all content going out, regardless of type. This is exactly how professional tools like CoSchedule and Hootsuite Planner work.

Here's what that looks like:

CALENDAR VIEW
├── 📱 Social Post chip  →  blue
├── 📄 Article chip      →  purple
│
├── Past date + published   →  🟢 green chip
├── Past date + NOT published → 🔴 red chip (missed!)
└── Future date + scheduled  →  ⏰ grey chip

On auto-publishing articles to Insights: This is a powerful and correct feature. One critical safeguard I'd recommend: auto-publish should only trigger when the article status is fully approved AND a publish date is set. A pre-publish checklist gate (title, featured image, SEO slug confirmed) prevents accidental live posts. Think of it like a "rocket launch commit" — the date is set, approvals are done, the system fires automatically.

✅ Q2 — Ideas Bank as home for Social Posts too?
My honest take: Merge them, but with smart filters — not two separate pages.

You're right to question why we'd have both. Here's the cleaner model:

IDEAS BANK  (/studio/ideas)
= One unified content library with filters
Filter by TYPE:   💡 Idea  |  📱 Social Post  |  📄 Article
Filter by STATUS: Draft → In Review → Approved → Scheduled → Published
💡 Idea cards      →  not yet activated, show [Start Creating] action
📱 Social Post cards → full lifecycle here, show platform + format badges
📄 Article cards   →  just a summary link (articles have their own editor)

Why this works: Social posts don't need a deep editor like articles do. Their lifecycle is short — generate, review, schedule. They live well in a card/list view. Ideas and social posts are "lightweight content." Articles are heavyweight and need their own editorial environment. So the split becomes:

Ideas Bank = home for ideas + social posts (lightweight, card-based)
Articles = home for long-form editorial work (heavy, editor-based)
✅ Q3 — Task Sequence Explained in Plain English
Here's what needs to be fixed and why in that order:

Step 1 — Fix how the Calendar works
Right now when you click "New Idea" on the Calendar, it either orphans the idea or bounces you to Articles. We fix this first so the Calendar becomes a proper planning surface — ideas flow correctly, posts show in green/red by publish status, articles show alongside social posts. This is the foundation everything else sits on.

Step 2 — Build the Ideas Bank page
Once Calendar is creating ideas correctly, those ideas need somewhere to live and be managed. We build /studio/ideas as the unified home — ideas, social posts, their full lifecycle in one filterable list.

Step 3 — Platform-specific AI for Social Posts
Right now AI generates generic content. We add platform selection (Instagram / LinkedIn / Facebook / X) + format selection (Carousel / Reel / Static / Story) so the AI output is actually usable. This lives inside the Ideas Bank / Social Post creation flow.

Step 4 — Fix Articles: CM Review + Author Sign-off
These two steps are currently broken. We repair the editorial pipeline so articles can actually complete their approval workflow and get to "Approved / Ready to Publish" status — which is also what enables the auto-publish on date feature.

Step 5 — Campaign AI brief generation
