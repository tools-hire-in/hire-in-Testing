# HIRE'IN APPROVED PROOF LIBRARY
*The file that turns [NEEDS_PROOF] flags into confident, insider content. Fill this in FIRST — before writing any content. One hour with your delivery lead is enough for the first ten cards.*

**How the AI must use this file:** Any claim about Hire'in's process, capabilities, results, clients, or credentials may ONLY be made if a card below supports it, and only within that card's approved context. Cite the card ID inline in drafts, e.g. *(PC-03)*. If no card covers a claim, flag **[NEEDS_PROOF: suggested card]** so the team knows exactly what to add. Cards marked EXAMPLE are placeholders — never use them in real content.

## PART 1 — THE EXTRACTION INTERVIEW (do this first)

Sit down with whoever runs delivery/recruiting and answer these. Every honest, specific answer becomes a proof card. Skip any you can't answer confidently — a short true library beats a long shaky one.

**Identity & footprint**
1. What roles/specialties has Hire'in actually placed in the last 12 months? (List them — this alone kills 30% of NEEDS_PROOF flags.)
2. Which states / regions / facility types do you actively serve today?
3. What employment models do you actually offer (travel, per diem, perm, W-2 contract, C2C, contract-to-hire)?

**Healthcare process**
4. Before submitting a nurse or allied professional, what do you verify — exactly? (License? Which certs? References — how many, from whom? Skills checklist? Background/drug screen timing?)
5. What does your intake call with a facility actually cover that a req alone doesn't?
6. What happens between "offer accepted" and "first shift" — who does what?

**IT/engineering process**
7. Before a CV goes to a client, what screening happens — exactly? (Recruiter screen? Technical conversation? Scenario/portfolio review? Who conducts it?)
8. How do you calibrate a technical role at intake — what do you ask that others don't?
9. What's your policy on right-to-represent / candidate consent before submission?

**Credentials, memberships, numbers**
10. Does Hire'in hold any certifications (e.g., Joint Commission HCSS), memberships, diversity certifications (MBE/WBE), or insurance levels worth naming?
11. What real numbers can you stand behind with a source? (Placements made, redeployment rate, average time-to-submit, submittal-to-interview ratio, consultant retention.) Only numbers someone can produce a report for.
12. Do you have ANY client or candidate who has given written permission to be named or quoted? Any written testimonial with documented consent?

**People**
13. Who at Hire'in has domain credibility worth citing? (e.g., "our clinical lead is a former ICU nurse," "our IT practice lead spent 10 years as an engineer.") Get their permission to be referenced.

## PART 2 — PROOF CARD FORMAT

Every card must have all five fields. No source + approver = not a card.

```
ID: PC-##
CLAIM (exact approved wording): ...
APPROVED CONTEXT (where/how it may be used): ...
SOURCE: (document, report, SOP, signed consent — something a person could produce)
APPROVED BY / DATE / EXPIRES: name — date — review date
```

## PART 5 — MAINTENANCE RULES

- Review expiry dates monthly; expired cards are treated as nonexistent by the AI.
- A restricted term ("fastest," "fully compliant," "top 1%," etc.) still requires a card that approves that exact wording — a related card is not enough.
- Named clients and testimonials require the consent document referenced in SOURCE, not a verbal OK.
- When a card changes, bump a version note in the card — content approved under the old wording shouldn't be silently orphaned.
