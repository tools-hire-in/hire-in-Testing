Validation rules
use full legal name exactly as in records
use dates in DD/MM/YYYY
document must be on official letterhead
signatory required before issue
seal/stamp optional but recommended
PDF should be immutable after issue
QR or verification ID strongly recommended
no blank placeholders allowed at issuance
no salary field in experience letter output
