Annexure A: 90-Day Growth Review Plan
1. Purpose

The 90-day growth review is intended to evaluate readiness for any future compensation revision based on measurable performance, retained business outcomes, candidate quality, profitability, ownership, communication, confidentiality, and healthcare desk contribution.

This review does not guarantee an automatic salary increment. Any future revision will remain subject to performance, business position, retained revenue, and management approval.

2. Review Period

Review Start Date: [Date]
Review End Date: [Date]
Review Duration: 90 days

3. Primary Review Standard

The primary focus of the review will be retained business outcomes, not submission volume alone.

Given the active healthcare job volume, existing pipeline, and available sourcing support, the expected 90-day target is:

Target: 10 qualified retained starts in 90 days
Expected monthly pace: 3–4 qualified retained starts per month

This target is subject to active client demand, open requirements, role flow, and company-approved profitability requirements.

Submission activity will be reviewed as a supporting metric only. The primary review factors will be retained starts, candidate quality, falloff control, profitability, billing continuity, RTR/documentation accuracy, reporting discipline, sourcer coordination, confidentiality, and overall healthcare desk ownership.

4. Definition of Qualified Retained Start

A candidate will count as a qualified retained start only when all of the following conditions are met:

Candidate starts the assignment.
Candidate is accepted by the client/MSP.
Candidate has complete RTR and required documentation.
Candidate meets role requirements and experience expectations.
Candidate meets pay, availability, location, license/specialty, and commitment requirements.
Placement meets company-approved margin/profitability requirements.
Candidate does not fall off due to avoidable no-show, poor screening, experience mismatch, commitment issue, documentation issue, or preventable follow-up gap.
Candidate is actively billing and retained as per company/client expectations.
