# Approval Rule

A card becomes **Approved** only when:

- The evidence is confirmed as current
- The permitted wording is accepted
- The restrictions are accepted
- An authorized owner enters their name and approval date

Use this sign-off format:

```text
Approval status: Approved
Approved by: [Name]
Approval date: [YYYY-MM-DD]
```

Until signed, keep the status as:

```text
Approval status: Ready for approval
```

# What These Cards Do Not Yet Prove

The following still require separate evidence:

- Named clients, including NVIDIA or healthcare organizations
- Placement counts
- Fill rates
- Submission-to-interview conversion
- Time-to-submit or time-to-fill
- Nationwide delivery
- 24/7 responsiveness
- Joint Commission, HIPAA, FISMA, FedRAMP, HUB, MBE, or other compliance/certification claims
- KlerHire product performance
- Client testimonials
- Specific public-sector contract access
- Quantified business outcomes

These claims must remain blocked or use `[NEEDS_PROOF]` until separate cards are approved.
