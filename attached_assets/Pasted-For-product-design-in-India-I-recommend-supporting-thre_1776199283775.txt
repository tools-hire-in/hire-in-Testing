Business rules I would build into the product
Employee letters should mention role, tenure, brief responsibilities, and a neutral-positive conduct line.
Intern letters should mention department, duration, project or assignment, skills learned, and whether the internship was successfully completed.
Salary, disciplinary history, and resignation reason should stay out of the experience letter unless your company has a deliberate policy to include something specific.
Use full legal names exactly as in records.
Use exact dates in DD/MM/YYYY.
Keep the standard template to one page.
Put the letter on official letterhead, with authorized signature and preferably a seal/stamp for India-style verification comfort.
Best default wording style for India

Use a tone that is:

formal
factual
positive but not overhyped
easy for HR verification teams to read

Avoid:

long praise paragraphs
vague dates
salary/CTC
personal opinions
reasons for leaving
anything that sounds like a recommendation unless you intentionally want that
