# SEO Strategy

## In scope
- Public marketing pages
- Service pages
- Guides and FAQ pages
- Public jobs listing and job detail pages
- Public contact, contracts, request-a-quote, privacy, and terms pages

## Out of scope
- Authenticated dashboard routes under `/admin/**`
- Employee-subdomain internal HR portal routes
- Tokenized acceptance flows such as `/onboard/:token` and `/addendum/:token`

## Target audience
- Employers hiring in Healthcare, IT, Engineering, and Professional Services
- Job seekers looking for staffing and contract opportunities

- AI recruitment
- healthcare staffing
- IT staffing
- engineering recruitment
- contract staffing
